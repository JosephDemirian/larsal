# FEA-10 Show as QR Code

Status: revised DRAFT (2026-11-04), after fresh second Spec SEND BACK; revised Spec review and fresh independent Verify pending
Epics: E21-S1 (this card and proposals only); E21-S2, E21-S3, E21-S4, E21-S5 after review and their board dependencies
Parity rows: SR-07, SR-13, SC-05, SD-07, SD-09; proposed extensions, not new verification claims

## What it does

Shows the one value the user selected as an ordinary QR symbol, locally, so another device can scan it. A separate action on a Wi-Fi entry makes a join code. A QR image is readable secret material, not encryption. Anyone with the picture can recover its content. The picture can be saved or shared only after a warning on every attempt (LD-336, LD-337 (1), (2)). Encrypted links are E21-S6, outside this card.

This draft does not authorize an implementation story. E21-S1 can start without an already-reviewed card; its successors cannot build from this draft. The Lead amendment rulings govern this revision; proposed LD-359 to LD-362 and exact E21-S1/S2/S4/S5 aftertexts are bound outside the checkout for joint integration. They are not adopted central records. Original acceptance stays unpassed until the Lead records the card, board and decisions together; unproved functionality stays required and OPEN. No new general owner-approval gate is introduced. The locked Option A files stay locked. Proposed frames are not owner-approved; the existing one-pass proposal review and any still-pending owner choices stay pending (LD-268, W-18).

## Inputs and outputs

Input: an unlocked vault/session, one entry and one field selected by the user, or an explicit Wi-Fi join selection. Output: one square module matrix including its quiet zone, its dimension and a non-secret purpose label, or a typed error. The matrix is secret-equivalent. It is not a URL to a service, a file, an attachment, clipboard content or a stored preview. No master key, password text or full entry record is needed by the renderer.

Owners of related behavior: [MOD-01](../model/MOD-01-entry-fields.md) fields; [MOD-08](../model/MOD-08-references-placeholders.md) Show resolution; [FEA-02](FEA-02-password-generator.md) and [FEA-03](FEA-03-passphrases.md) generator limits; [FEA-04.23](FEA-04-one-time-passwords.md) authenticator setup QR; [APP-02](../app/APP-02-lock.md) lock; [APP-08](../app/APP-08-menus-shortcuts.md) menus, shortcuts and access; [APP-10](../app/APP-10-screen-protection.md) window guard; [INT-10.28](../integrations/INT-10-command-line.md) command-line Show; [INT-11](../integrations/INT-11-browser-extension.md) extension authorization. No WEB card exists at this base: E20 and [browser-and-web](../research/browser-and-web.md) B15, B19 to B22, B26 are the current web contract, including WEB-02's intended strict CSP.

## Rules

FEA-10.1 **One deliberate action.** ACT-QR-SHOW is visible beside the Password in the entry view, and beside every protected standard/custom text field, masked or already shown, including protected notes. It is enabled for one existing nonempty value eligible under that frontend's Show and the authority table below, in an unlocked vault; no bulk QR, hover reveal or automatic display. Missing/empty fields cannot reveal a previous matrix. Programmatic calls still check the field and session. A focused editor field may show its current unsaved text without saving it; the Rust draft is the source. This does not add actions to master-key, account-key, passkey or other non-exportable credential controls.

FEA-10.2 **Every entry point.** The entry list's selected-row actions, Entry menu, entry view's More actions and entry-row context menu expose ACT-QR-SHOW for that entry's Password. Right-clicking a protected field targets that field, never a fallback password. Proposed default shortcut is ⌃⌥⌘Q on Mac, scoped to the active Larsal window: a focused protected field wins, otherwise the one selected entry's Password. No selection, multiple selection, locked session or open unrelated sheet disables it. APP-08 custom shortcut conflict and reserved-key rules still apply. The extension popup adds the same labelled action to each login row and to its entry Password and every protected field it exposes; the web vault does likewise in its entry list/view. If a protected field is not otherwise exposed, an entry's “Show Field as QR Code” submenu lists its name and acts on that one field, without fetching every value. Browser versions offer the shortcut while their own page has focus, subject to browser-reserved keys; no global command, page keystroke or content script can reveal it.

FEA-10.3 **Exact authorized Show result.** For a saved value use the existing frontend's authorized Show resolution result, in its existing context, including missing-span, depth and work-limit behavior (MOD-08.18 to .20/.25, LD-170/172). For a draft use its current literal Rust draft value. Encode that result's exact UTF-8 bytes: no label, field name, newline, prefix, BOM, normalization, trimming, case change, HTML escape, replacement character, base64, compression or envelope. A Show operation that actually returns an error produces no QR and uses its existing error. Successful raw fallback text is not a new resolver error: nonempty fallback is capacity checked and encoded exactly; empty text gives MSG-QR-EMPTY. Never concatenate partial internal work or newly expose a field that frontend's Show excludes. Generic-field whitespace, LF, CR, NUL and controls remain data bytes. Saved `{USERNAME}` with demo username `demo-user` gives `demo-user`; a draft containing `{USERNAME}` gives literal `{USERNAME}`. No execution/navigation of decoded URL/markup text by Larsal is authorized; this does not disable saved-entry resolution.

Eligibility is the existing frontend's authorized one-value Show eligibility and resolution context. The QR action changes presentation only. It adds no content-based or private-field classification, and does not refuse data that the same frontend already authorizes the user to Show. Master-key, account-key, passkey-provider and other non-exportable controls that are not eligible for that frontend's Show remain excluded. Native saved/draft exclusions and CLI's explicitly requested broader eligibility remain as recorded below. Sensitive implementation review must name the narrow matrix export and its ownership; it does not defer or change this eligibility contract. Existing text Show is unchanged.

| Frontend/source | Existing authority to preserve | QR restriction |
|---|---|---|
| Native saved entry | `show_field`/`check_field`: one field, excluding one-time-password and passkey fields; shared Copy resolution context suppresses references to them (LD-170 (4)) | Same authorized result, never a direct excluded field or the suppressed reference's raw secret |
| Native draft | `show_draft_field` gives literal custom/additional-URL/standard text; Password uses its separate Show path | Use the selected Rust draft, including its revision; no saved-entry resolution or full draft reply |
| CLI | INT-10.28/LD-172: explicit one `-a` gives full resolution, including legacy OTP/passkey-field eligibility; default Password requires `-s`; `--totp` requests only the current code | Preserve INT-10.28/LD-172's explicit selected-field context, including its existing OTP/passkey-field eligibility; do not borrow native `check_field` or introduce a new private-content refusal. This adds no access to key controls that the CLI's existing Show does not expose. |
| Extension / web | Current INT-01/02/11 and E20/B19/B26 allow only the requested value under their policies, not every native/CLI field | Add only a reviewed selected-matrix request; no inferred whole-entry or options-page access, no access to non-exportable controls outside that frontend's Show |

The standalone historical D8 decision text is missing from the supplied/current README authority. LD-197's stale “D8 to D11” pointer is disclosed, not reconstructed. Current AGENTS, architecture and LD-155/156/197 govern; no missing decision grants access.

FEA-10.4 **Wi-Fi selection and fields.** ACT-QR-WIFI appears beside a Wi-Fi entry's network/security information and in its context menu; its Password's ACT-QR-SHOW still gives only the password. The join action explicitly authorizes the configuration's fields as one requested picture. Reuse `UserName` for SSID, `Password` for the credential and `Security` for the type. Custom fields (required for the selected enterprise profile where .6 says so): `Hidden` (absent = false; exact `true`/`false`), `EAP Method`, `Identity`, `Anonymous Identity`, `Phase 2 Method`. Read these stored values literally, as the existing Wi-Fi path does; no placeholder resolution, guessed SSID from Title, probing of the current network or saved change. SSID must be nonempty; control characters U+0000 to U+001F and U+007F in join fields, malformed Hidden or missing required enterprise fields give MSG-QR-WIFI-INVALID. Unknown security is never treated as WPA.

FEA-10.5 **Wi-Fi wire format.** Use the maintained [Wi-Fi content convention](https://github.com/zxing/zxing/wiki/Barcode-Contents#wi-fi-network-config-android-ios-11), not a new protocol. Larsal's stable order is `WIFI:T:‹type›;S:‹ssid›;P:‹credential›;H:‹boolean›;;`; open omits P. Enterprise inserts E/I, then supplied A/PH2, after H before the extra terminator. Escape all five original special characters (backslash, semicolon, comma, double quote, colon) once in each string field. Only S/P get outer literal quote wrappers for nonempty even-length ASCII hex-looking input; add these after escaping their original characters. Odd-length hex-looking S/P stay unwrapped. An original quote is escaped, distinct from a wrapper. I/A `ABCD` remain `ABCD`, without added quotes. Other string fields E/I/A/PH2 are escaped once without wrappers; T is the selected wire token, H a generated `true`/`false`. Preserve spaces and Unicode; no URL/percent encoding or resolution. Capacity counts the final UTF-8 including keys, delimiters, escapes and wrappers. Field order, hex-pair predicate and custom metadata names are explicit Larsal choices; the convention does not define an exhaustive enterprise support matrix. The payload stays local until explicit export.

FEA-10.6 **Network profiles and required proof.** Exact Security values `WPA`, `WPA Personal`, `WPA2 Personal`, `WPA/WPA2 Personal`, `WPA2/WPA3 Personal` map to T `WPA`; `WEP` to `WEP`; `Open`/`nopass` to `nopass`. Offer the separate join action from that explicit mapping, including open networks the current classifier misses. Missing Security is invalid, not implicitly open. Never use the existing non-WEP-to-WPA default as evidence of support. All rows allow Hidden absent/false/true as .4 specifies. Ordinary field QR stays separate.

| Profile | Required fields / optional fields | Proposed payload and trust / acceptance boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Listed personal aliases; WEP | Nonempty UserName, Password, exact Security; optional Hidden | S/P literal escaping and T mapping above. Transition-mode compatibility is required device proof, not a claim of SAE-only support |
| Open / nopass | Nonempty UserName, exact Security; optional Hidden | T:nopass, no P even with stale stored Password; no secure-network claim |
| Selected enterprise serialization profile: Security `WPA2-EAP`, EAP Method `TTLS`, Phase 2 Method `MSCHAPV2` | Nonempty UserName (SSID), Identity, Password and those three exact selector fields; optional Anonymous Identity and Hidden as .4 specifies | T:WPA2-EAP, E:TTLS, PH2:MSCHAPV2, with I and supplied A, using .5's escaping and stable order. This selects the encoder contract only, not a supported receiver or a passed Join check |
| Other password enterprise profiles, including PEAP, PWD or absent phase 2 | Their exact phase-2/identity requirements are not established as a Larsal join contract | Retain as unresolved required support for Lead review; do not accept arbitrary nonempty E/PH2 as proof. No silent narrowing of the planned join capability or default method |
| SAE-only `WPA3 Personal`, OWE, certificate-only / client-certificate enterprise including TLS, unknown type/method | Unsupported by this proposed payload subset; missing required fields and malformed Hidden are INVALID | UNSUPPORTED where type/profile cannot be represented by the proposed subset. This is a current proposal limit, not a claim those networks can never use QR onboarding; any required broader support stays OPEN |

[Apple enterprise settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/deployment/dynamic-wep-wpa-enterprise-wpa2-settings-dep1c4b37ac2/1) require EAP and server trust; [Android enterprise configuration](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/connectivity/wifi-enterprise) requires CA and server-name validation for TLS-based profiles on its documented API paths. Those are configuration contracts, not proof of camera-QR behavior. The convention does not carry those trust settings. Enterprise Join stays unavailable until independent device proof accepts a named scanner/OS/version for the selected profile. It must preserve TTLS/MSCHAPV2, use independently provisioned correct CA and server-name validation, and refuse wrong/untrusted servers without personal/open fallback. Test both Hidden values and every claimed Anonymous Identity/TLS variant; optional-on-wire does not mean supported on a receiver. Existing trust must be shown to remain effective through the actual scan/configuration path, never assumed from a prior network entry. Larsal introduces no certificate installation, trust-on-first-use enrollment, trust-disable prompt, network probe, new onboarding protocol or downgrade. A decoded string, scan prompt, existing trusted profile or UNSUPPORTED response is not a successful Join. Other enterprise methods and broader phone-join support remain required OPEN work; this selected first profile neither removes them nor passes the original broader acceptance. The current [AOSP parser](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/modules/Wifi/+/refs/heads/main/framework/java/android/net/wifi/WifiUriParser.java) reads no E/I/A/PH2 and uses a WPA-prefix personal branch: source inspection predicts misclassification of WPA2-EAP on that route, not safe enterprise support or a device result. [Apple's profile schema](https://github.com/apple/device-management/blob/release/mdm/profiles/com.apple.wifi.managed.yaml) defines trust and TLS/OuterIdentity requirements separately from QR scanning. No enterprise receiver is approved by these sources.

FEA-10.7 **Existing OTP code unchanged.** ACT-OTP-QR remains FEA-04.23's separate authenticator setup action: form U URI from current settings and unresolved title/username; smallest fitting normal version, byte mode, H, four-module quiet zone, MSG-OTP-QR-CUSTOM for custom/Steam, MSG-OTP-QR-ERROR then close if too long, 44-second countdown and close on lock. Its URI Copy button and Cmd+C remain under APP-03. Do not replace it with the current six-digit code, generic field text, a Wi-Fi code or a link; do not add these new Save/Share actions to that existing screen in E21. FEA-10's no-clipboard rule is for the new actions, not removal of the existing OTP Copy. The capture limit in .21 applies to any displayed secret, including the existing OTP screen; it changes no OTP content or generation rule.

FEA-10.8 **Encoding and verified Unicode.** New value/join codes use normal QR versions 1 to 40, error correction H, one ECI designator 26 (UTF-8) followed by one byte-mode segment, with the smallest version that fits. No Micro QR, automatic numeric/alphanumeric/Kanji switching, structured append or silent fallback to a lower correction level. [ISO public scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/83389.html), [originator's versions](https://www.qrcode.com/en/about/version.html), and the installed qrcode 0.14.1 `Bits` API support these choices. The installed API has `push_eci_designator`, `push_byte_data`, `push_terminator`, `QrCode::with_bits`, `width`, `to_colors`; ECI is not automatically supplied by the current ffi byte-only helper. Preserve the OTP route's existing bitstream (.7). Verify must test ECI 26 *and* raw recovered bytes: a successful ASCII scan alone does not prove Unicode. This is a proposed encoding ruling, not evidence that Core Image or every camera has already passed it.

FEA-10.9 **Hard capacity, no truncation.** At version 40 H there are 10,208 data bits; the published byte-only maximum is 1,273 bytes ([originator's table](https://www.qrcode.com/en/about/versionPage/versionPage31_40.html)). ECI 26 costs 12 bits, and byte mode plus the version-40 count costs 20: floor((10,208 − 12 − 20) / 8) = **1,272 UTF-8 payload bytes**. Check the bound after resolution/join escaping and before encoder allocation. At 1,273 or more return MSG-QR-TOO-LARGE and no matrix/file/share. Invalid encoding or allocation/encoder failure gives MSG-QR-FAILED, never a partial or previous symbol. Generator, imported-field and vault-format limits stay unchanged. This refusal defines safe behavior; it does **not** pass the original longest-generator acceptance. The Lead capacity choice requires the formal joint amendment described in D-10-1; no generator limit changes, fallback or truncation are authorized.

FEA-10.10 **Matrix boundary and lifetime.** Reuse the pinned, default-features-off qrcode matrix path in Rust, not an image/network service or JavaScript QR package. LD-198's current importer restriction is ffi `edit.rs` `qr()` only. The proposed LD-361 retains it: no new importer, CLI/wasm scope, ABI or allocator grant before sensitive review names the exact site, target, API class and wiping owner; keep image/svg/pic disabled. On Mac and in the extension's native host, selection, resolution, join construction and encoding happen in Rust secret types under the existing zeroing allocator. Return only the requested matrix/dimension to the authorized renderer, never the source text. This is the narrow QR-picture class of LD-220, not permission for arbitrary entry records to cross. New export names must be named in API.md and the lead's LD-220 clarification before implementation. On wasm, require independent per-target proof of private input/working-bit/codeword/matrix wiping on every release, including before session lock; native allocator evidence and a session-wide wipe cannot prove earlier freed private buffers. Never link ffi/platform into wasm to borrow native wiping. JS must clear/drop its bounded matrix/pixel buffers and retain the existing session lock wipe. No secret Debug, Clone, errors, console, analytics or crash strings. Drop all app-owned copies at dismissal; Swift/JS/system/GPU copies cannot be promised to be securely erased by dropping a reference.

FEA-10.11 **Popup and animation.** One popup/dialog per session/window, title MSG-QR-TITLE, purpose, image, exposure/capture lines, countdown, then Save Image…, Share…, Done. Done is initial/default focus. Opening a different QR destroys the old one; no stack, detachable window, keep-open setting or underlying revealed text. Reuse Option A parts. Enter uses Done; Escape, window close, click outside or new selection dismisses. Animate only dialog chrome over proposed 160 ms: opacity 0 to 1, vertical offset 6 pt to 0, ease-out. Reserve image geometry, but paint no code during entry; its first complete frame is fully opaque, settled at the final backing-pixel-aligned origin. Never fade, translate, scale, rotate or interpolate code modules, including intermediate frames. Start the original 44-second deadline at that first complete code frame. Reduce Motion paints immediately and remains stationary. A live Reduce Motion change stops chrome motion and settles immediately; if the first code has not appeared, that settled frame starts its deadline, otherwise the original deadline never restarts. Dismissal clears pixels immediately, without a secret-bearing exit animation. Sources: [Apple popovers](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/popovers), [Reduce Motion](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/environmentvalues/accessibilityreducemotion), [WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/7551/responsive-design-for-motion/). Exact timings are proposed policy, not platform mandates.

FEA-10.12 **Size and contrast.** N includes four clear white modules per edge: N = 17 + 4v + 8, at most 185. Square black (#000000) cells on solid white (#FFFFFF) in both themes; no transparency, tint, rounded cells, logo, border/text in quiet zone or interpolation. Code contrast is 21:1; surrounding tokens/focus follow Option A. Prefer 296 pt; at backing scale d choose integer physical pitch s = floor(296d/N), minimum 3. If s < 3 grow to N×3 physical pixels if available. Final panel is exactly Ns/d points; round its origin to integer backing pixels before painting. A v40 code at 1× needs 555 physical pixels: with Option A's existing 22+22 pt padding, propose popup outer width **599 pt**, preserving controls and enough vertical space for the whole dialog. Locked 460 pt content is 416; even locked 560 pt wide content is 516, so neither fits that dense 1× panel. At 2× pitch 3 needs 277.5 pt, which fits the ordinary 460 pt dialog; half-point coordinates are allowed only when they are integral physical pixels. Recompute size/origin on screen, scale and bounds changes; no scaled secret transition. If the full code and controls cannot fit, remove all code pixels, show MSG-QR-SIZE and disable exports. Capacity refusal (1273 bytes) is distinct from presentation-size refusal (a valid fitting payload with insufficient display bounds). Compact browser bounds follow the same rule; never crop or scroll only the symbol. Dense/growth/SIZE illustrations below are draft proposals, not changes to locked geometry. The readback corpus uses adequate presentation space. Pitches are policy, not universal scan-distance guarantees ([four-module margin](https://www.qrcode.com/en/howto/code.html/index.html), [correction tradeoff](https://www.qrcode.com/en/about/error_correction.html)).

FEA-10.13 **Keyboard and accessibility.** Dialog has a name and modal semantics; background is inert while it is open. Tab/Shift-Tab cycle Save Image…, Share… (if available), Done; full keyboard access and visible focus ring follow APP-08.25. The image is one element named “QR code for ‹purpose›”; its accessible label, help, tooltip, DOM attributes and hidden text contain neither payload nor a textual transcript. VoiceOver announces the title, exposure/capture lines and initial expiry once; the countdown is available to read but does not interrupt speech every second. Do not automatically speak an already-shown value. On close, restore focus to the invoker if it still exists, otherwise the entry list (unlock control on lock). The warning focuses Cancel, traps focus and returns to its triggering button if cancelled. Long field names wrap as plain text; large text must not overlap/crop controls. Each new scan needs a fresh action after expiry, keeping the existing 44-second secret-exposure bound; access never requires scanning or a mouse to operate Save/Share/Done.

FEA-10.14 **Expiry and request identity.** Establish one 44-second deadline at the first complete displayed code frame (.11). Show ceil(remaining seconds): 44 … 1, then clear/dismiss at zero. Use elapsed time/deadlines, never callback counts. Sleep, stalled/background timers, resize, warning/system UI, activity and refocus cannot extend it. Bind every result, consent and callback to vault/session identity, entry/field or Wi-Fi configuration identity, saved-source revision or editor-draft revision, invoker and request generation. Same-entry edits, merge/sync/reload and draft edits invalidate stale data even if selection IDs stay equal. Before every repaint, export operation or lazy data-provider callback, check that identity, revision, revocation and the original deadline. Discard late mismatched results; no automatic regeneration/restoration. The timer is not capture detection and cannot force execution while the OS suspends the process.

FEA-10.15 **Lock requests and visibility.** Invalidate the QR/capability at every real source-vault lock request immediately, before APP-02.15 steps 3/4 wait for reload/save or steps 7/8 ask about edits/data. Step 5 alone is too late for this stronger QR rule. Even a later-cancelled manual lock never restores it. Also revoke on close, quit, sleep, screen lock/user switch, app hide, miniaturize, order-out, hidden Space/window or unrelated deactivation/occlusion; apply with lock-on-minimize off or delayed locking. Visibility is not capture detection. The intentional transition to the user's explicitly confirmed save/share system UI is distinct: clear on-screen pixels before transition and retain only the still-live action-bound export capability under .16; expected blur/occlusion from that UI alone does not revoke it. Any unrelated app/hide/source change still revokes app-owned work. No QR restoration after system export cancellation/completion; only dismissal of an app-owned warning may return to its still-live original popup without restarting time. Extension popup close/disconnect/navigation/lock revokes; web hidden/pagehide/unrelated blur/session loss/trap does likewise. BFCache return never restores. Because suspension can delay callbacks, recheck .14 before any repaint/export. Sources: [Apple occlusion](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appkit/nswindow/didchangeocclusionstatenotification), [visibility limits](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/Performance/Conceptual/power_efficiency_guidelines_osx/WorkWhenVisible.html), [HTML visibility/focus](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/interaction.html#page-visibility).

FEA-10.16 **Per-attempt consent and earliest handoff.** Save warns with MSG-QR-SAVE-TITLE and MSG-QR-EXPORT-WARNING; Share warns with MSG-QR-SHARE-TITLE and both warning lines. Cancel is default; Return/Escape/window close cancel app-owned warning work with no output. Confirmation is single-use, bound to .14's identities/revisions, current matrix, action and original deadline. Every retry, double click, changed source or other action needs a fresh warning; no remembered consent or alternate API/menu/drag bypass. While readable data is exclusively Larsal-controlled, lock/hide/source change/expiry invalidates consent, cancels app-owned warning/panel/picker work, destroys the on-screen code and prevents a new write/handoff. Recheck liveness immediately before export encoding, file commit and any platform API receiving readable image data. That **earliest receiving API** is the conservative external-custody boundary, including picker initialization/presentation if it receives the item; never use a later target-selection or successful-send callback instead. After handoff, clear/release Larsal copies and close UI it can control, but promise no platform cancellation, compose/send veto, copy erasure or recall. Every later lazy provider callback must check the original deadline/revocation and fail closed; this cannot revoke bytes already obtained by the platform. Move a native handoff boundary later only with specific public-API and runtime proof. No runtime cancellation proof exists in this draft.

FEA-10.17 **Save Image.** Only after confirmation and an explicit destination does Larsal write one lossless PNG named `Larsal QR.png` by default (never a password, field value or entry title as filename). Export pure black/white pixels with quiet zone at exactly 8 pixels/module, no metadata, transcript, vault path or thumbnail sidecar. Owner-only 0600 on Mac; reuse safe export/overwrite handling and refuse symlink destinations rather than following them. Cancellation before any write makes no Larsal file; revoke before committing a still-owned save. After browser/download image handoff apply .16's external-custody limit, not a universal cancellation promise. Do not pre-render into a disk cache, temp file, autosave, drag file, clipboard or attachment. Browser explicit downloads are saves: the chosen browser/OS destination and permissions apply, so no 0600 or secure-deletion promise there. A new download request still needs the warning; no automatic retry. The original deadline never restarts, and no QR is restored after the system save flow.

FEA-10.18 **Share, app-call scope and open capability.** Only explicit warned Share may hand an in-memory image to the system/browser and then a user-chosen destination. Larsal must not write/request a staging file, silently save, upload directly or invoke clipboard operations. Exclude known Copy/clipboard, Print, automatic Save and Larsal-staging-file targets. Use .16's earliest boundary and the exact retention warning before every attempt.

Disabled/unavailable Share is interim behavior, never successful Share. ## How this was learned

This revision admitted the first Spec source and preserved its proof read-only, then read current Larsal contracts and the fresh Spec2/Lead rulings. No outside product/reference code, reference app/CLI, vault or keychain was opened. Public primary documentation was checked read-only on2026-11-04, with extraction limits recorded externally; no paid standard was purchased. First Spec research remains historical, not a new implementation test. ISO's public [2024 edition description](https://www.iso.org/standard/83389.html) gives scope, not the unavailable full normative text. Originator pages and the installed bit table support the numerical/scaling claims; exact screen pitches/time/shortcut are proposed Larsal policy.

Primary product precedents: [Proton's Wi-Fi sharing guidance](https://proton.me/blog/how-to-share-wifi-password) describes phone QR joining and the risk of unencrypted sharing; [Bitwarden's integrated authenticator](https://bitwarden.com/help/integrated-authenticator/) treats OTP setup separately. Those do not prove a universal arbitrary-password QR feature or this implementation's correctness. Existing LD-198/220 and browser-and-web research supply the established Rust/matrix and client-trust decisions. The [qrcode maintainer's API](https://docs.rs/qrcode/latest/qrcode/struct.QrCode.html) identifies version 0.14.1; local `vendor/crates-io/qrcode-0.14.1/src/bits.rs` and `lib.rs` were the precise API inspected. Browser expiry/sharing rules use the HTML and W3C sources cited above; current Apple HIG/platform sources are linked at the rules they support. Sources containing disallowed names must remain external proof only; none is required in this card's citations.

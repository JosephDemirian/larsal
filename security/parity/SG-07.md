# SG-07 One-time codes

Row: SG-07 in [plan/security.md](../../plan/security.md). Story: E4-S10. Spec: FEA-04.
Date: 2026-10-26 (Sprint 3). Status: verified.

## Tests (all pass)

`crates/app-core/tests/fea04.rs` (29), among them `fea04_11_rfc_6238_and_rfc_4226_vectors`, `fea04_13_steam_code`, `fea04_09_base32_secret`, `fea04_05_percent_decoding`, `fea04_security_secret_never_in_debug_text`, `fea04_security_hostile_text_is_bounded`; `crates/app-crypto/tests/mac.rs` (HMAC-SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512 through app-crypto).

## Independent checks

- RFC 6238 table: SHA-1, SHA-256 and SHA-512 at the six times of the RFC (59 s to 20,000,000,000 s), read by App from a demo vault made by the reference command line tool: 18 of 18 codes equal in App, in Python's `hmac` and in the RFC.
- Side by side with the reference command line tool (`show --totp`, App's code taken just before and after each call, same window): every storage form and setting gives the same code, 16 of 16: the `otpauth` link with SHA-1, SHA-256 and SHA-512, 6, 8 and 10 digits, periods 30 and 60, the Steam encoder and a decoded `%45`; the key-query form with defaults, SHA-256 / 8 digits / 60 s and SHA-512; the two-field legacy form with 30;6, 30;S (Steam) and 60;8; the four read-only fields of another app with defaults, SHA-256 / 8 / 60 and SHA-512.
- Differences: an empty secret gives the reference's empty-key code and App's invalid key (D-04-1, on purpose); escapes of control characters in a link secret (`%00` to `%1F`, `%7F`) are kept as text by the reference and decoded by App, so the key differs (recorded in FEA-04.5 for a ruling; no real tool writes such a secret).

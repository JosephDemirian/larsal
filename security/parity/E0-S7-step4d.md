## 1. What changed

- **`vendor/ureq`** (new, ureq 3.5.0, MIT OR Apache-2.0): a `[patch.crates-io]` path copy with `Cargo.toml`, `LICENSE-APACHE`, `LICENSE-MIT` and `src/`. Each change is listed at the top of its `Cargo.toml` (changes 1 to 9) and the crate docs start with one note that the cut parts are gone:
  1. proxies: `src/proxy.rs`, `src/unversioned/transport/connect.rs` (HTTP CONNECT) and `socks.rs` cut; the `proxy` setting, its getter and builder, `Proxy::try_from_env()` in the default config, the proxy part of the pool key and the `ConnectProxyFailed` and `InvalidProxyUrl` errors gone. The connector chain is now `()`, then TCP, then rustls (and native-tls, both behind their features as before).
  2. cookies: `src/cookies.rs` cut; the agent's cookie jar, the cookie headers in `run.rs` and the three cookie errors gone.
  3. JSON: `send_json`, `SendBody::from_json`, the two `read_json` functions and the `Json` error gone.
  4. charset and the lossy reader: `src/body/charset.rs` and `src/body/lossy.rs` cut; the body reader passes bytes through unchanged (`CharsetDecoder::PassThrough`), as it did with the `charset` feature off, and the `UnknownCharset` error is gone.
  5. compression: `src/body/gzip.rs` and `src/body/brotli.rs` cut; a `gzip` or `br` content encoding passes through undecoded (`is_decompressing` is `false`), as it did with the `gzip` and `brotli` features off; no `accept-encoding` header is sent, as before; the `Decompress` error is gone.
  6. query and form helpers: `src/query.rs` cut; `query`, `query_raw`, `query_pairs`, `query_pairs_raw` and `send_form` gone.
  7. ureq's own tests: `src/test.rs` (the test transport), `src/io_timeout_tests.rs`, `src/shutdown_tests.rs` and every `#[cfg(test)]` module cut; the time and pool-count hooks the library itself calls stay.

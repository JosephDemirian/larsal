# E0-S7 step 4c: uniffi and uniffi_core as path copies (2026-10-31)

Builder, 2026-10-31. Story E0-S7 follow-up (plan/epics.md; LD-259 (1), LD-261 (1)), batch 4c of spec/research/dependencies.md section 8 'Step 4: trims', item 6: uniffi (drop the always-on `uniffi_pipeline` dependency, research F6) and uniffi_core (R14: `static_assertions` goes) become path copies in `vendor/uniffi` and `vendor/uniffi_core`, each change listed at the top of its `Cargo.toml`. Manifest: (1) the `static_assertions` dependency is gone; (2) the `readme` entry is gone, as the copy leaves out README.md (and the same four other files). `src/lib.rs`, 2 lines removed and 32 added (comments included), each place marked `App change (E0-S7 R14)`; its MPL-2.0 notice stays at the top:

  - both `#[doc(hidden)] #[macro_export]`; the crate's own `static_assertions::const_assert!(PACKAGE_VERSION.len() < 10);` now calls `deps::static_assertions::const_assert!`.
